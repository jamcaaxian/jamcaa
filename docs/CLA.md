## Why this agreement asks for relicensing rights

Section 2 grants the right to distribute Contributions under different licence terms. This is deliberate, and it is worth explaining rather than burying.

Without it, changing the Project's licence at any point in the future would require locating every past contributor and obtaining individual consent — which in practice makes the licence permanently unchangeable. Retaining this flexibility is a decision recorded in [ADR-0012](./adr/0012-apache-2-license-and-cla.md).

It does **not** mean Contributions will be taken proprietary. The Project is published under Apache-2.0, and any release already made under that licence remains available under it permanently — that cannot be revoked.
